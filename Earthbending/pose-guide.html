Earthbending pose guide

Empty hands, standing on natural ground. A pose fires as your hands arrive in it; you don't have to hold it. The dotted shapes are how far off a hand can be (33 cm sideways, 28 cm up or down, 36 cm in reach). Hand 1 and hand 2 can be either hand: every pose also works mirrored. For a stone, strike Base first, then go into the stone's pose within about a second.

Start here

Base

Arms a stone. Do this first, then go straight into a stone's pose.

  • Hand 1: belly, right, 22 cm out. Palm up, fingers forward.
  • Hand 2: belly, left, 22 cm out. Palm up, fingers forward.

Stones (Base first, then one of these)

Disc

Stone: a flat disc that flies off.

  • Hand 1: chest, right, 48 cm out. Palm left, fingers forward.
  • Hand 2: chest, left, 28 cm out. Palm back toward you, fingers right.

Ball

Stone: a ball that rolls away.

  • Hand 1: chest, right, 22 cm out. Palm left, fingers forward.
  • Hand 2: chest, left, 50 cm out. Palm right, fingers forward.

Pillar

Stone: a tall pillar.

  • Hand 1: belly, right, 28 cm out. Palm left, fingers forward.
  • Hand 2: eye level, left, 22 cm out. Palm right, fingers up.

Cube

Stone: a cube.

  • Hand 1: eye level, right, 20 cm out. Palm left, fingers up.
  • Hand 2: eye level, left, 20 cm out. Palm right, fingers up.

Wall

Stone: a wide wall.

  • Hand 1: above your head, right, 27 cm out. Palm up, fingers forward.
  • Hand 2: above your head, left, 27 cm out. Palm up, fingers forward.

Moves on a stone

Straight

Punches a stone straight ahead.

  • Hand 1: chest, right, 20 cm out. Palm left, fingers up.
  • Hand 2: chest, left, 50 cm out. Palm down, fingers forward.

Kick

Kicks a stone along the ground.

  • Hand 1: chest, right, 24 cm out. Palm left, fingers up.
  • Hand 2: belly, left, 22 cm out. Palm back toward you, fingers down.

Stomp

Drives a stone down.

  • Hand 1: belly, right, 35 cm out. Palm down and back toward you, fingers down and forward.
  • Hand 2: belly, left, 35 cm out. Palm down and back toward you, fingers down and forward.

Uppercut

Launches a stone up.

  • Hand 1: chest, right, 22 cm out. Palm left, fingers up and forward.
  • Hand 2: eye level, left, 33 cm out. Palm back toward you, fingers up.

Parry

Stops a stone coming at you.

  • Hand 1: chest, right, 45 cm out. Palm forward, fingers up.
  • Hand 2: chest, left, 45 cm out. Palm forward, fingers up.

Hold

Picks a stone up; swing to throw it.

  • Hand 1: chest, right, 42 cm out. Palm forward, fingers up.
  • Hand 2: chest, middle, 41 cm out. Palm up, fingers forward.

Flick

Holds a stone between your hands and turns it.

  • Hand 1: chest, right, 45 cm out. Palm right, fingers forward.
  • Hand 2: chest, right, 40 cm out. Palm right, fingers forward.

Explode

Marks a stone to blast when it breaks.

  • Hand 1: chest, right, 18 cm out. Palm back toward you, fingers left.
  • Hand 2: chest, left, 18 cm out. Palm back toward you, fingers right.

Moving yourself

Jump

Throws you up.

  • Hand 1: belly, right, 18 cm out. Palm up, fingers forward.
  • Hand 2: eye level, left, 6 cm out. Palm right, fingers up.

Dash

Throws you forward.

  • Hand 1: belly, right, 18 cm out. Palm up, fingers forward.
  • Hand 2: chest, left, 46 cm out. Palm right, fingers forward.
